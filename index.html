rclone (personal)

This is a private Google OAuth client used by one person to run rclone, the open-source command-line tool, against their own Google Drive.

It backs up and restores the owner's files between their computer and their Drive. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

The Google Drive scope, so rclone can list, upload, download and verify files in the owner's own Drive.
